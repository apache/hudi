Apache Hudi brings stream processing to big data, providing fresh data while being an order of magnitude more efficient than traditional batch processing. In a data lake or warehouse, Hudi tables support upserts, deletes and incremental queries; the timeline records every action (commits, cleans, compactions) as an instant, so readers see consistent snapshots. Embedding tables extend this: each PDF is split into chunks of at most 256 tokens with up to 128 tokens of overlap, and every chunk is identified by (fid, chunk_id). Numbers like 3.14159, URLs like https://hudi.apache.org/docs/overview, and non-ASCII text such as "naïve café — 数据湖" exercise the tokenizer's byte-level fallback.
